Cupuaçu Pudding

On this page, I'm going to show you how to make a typical Northern Brazilian dessert, with a little twist on the original recipe.

Ingredients

  • 1 can of sweetened condensed milk
  • 1 can of whole milk (use the same can to measure)
  • 3 eggs
  • 1 cup of cupuaçu pulp (preferably without added sugar)
  • 1 cup of sugar (for the caramel)

Instructions

  1. Prepare the caramel: In a small saucepan, melt 1 cup of sugar over medium heat until it turns golden brown. Quickly pour the caramel into a pudding mold (or bundt pan), spreading it evenly over the bottom. Set aside.
  2. Blend the mixture: In a blender, combine the sweetened condensed milk, whole milk, eggs, and cupuaçu pulp. Blend until smooth and creamy.
  3. Bake the pudding: Pour the mixture into the caramel-covered mold. Cover with aluminum foil and bake in a water bath (bain-marie) at 180°C (350°F) for about 1 hour, or until set.
  4. Let it cool completely, then refrigerate for at least 4 hours before unmolding and serving.
